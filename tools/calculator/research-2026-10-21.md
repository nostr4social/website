## 1. Storage per GB-month

- **S3 Standard us-east-1**: USD 0.023 (0-50 TB), 0.022 (50-500 TB), 0.021 (>500 TB), effective 2026-10-01. https://pricing.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/offers/v1.0/aws/AmazonS3/current/us-east-1/index.csv (the S3 pricing page is JS-rendered; the API is the authoritative list). Apply: default blob-storage rate; multiply stored GB per user (photos/video) by MAU.
- **GCS Standard**: USD 0.020 us-east1, 0.026 US multi-region (Google's own worked examples, https://cloud.google.com/storage/pricing-examples). Apply: alternate cloud toggle.
- **Backblaze B2**: USD 6.95/TB-month (0.00695/GB). https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-storage/pricing. Apply: "cheap tier" option.
- **Cloudflare R2**: USD 0.015/GB-month; Class A ops 4.50/M, Class B 0.36/M. https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/. Apply: "no-egress" option.
- **RDS PostgreSQL gp3 us-east-1**: USD 0.115 single-AZ, 0.23 Multi-AZ, 0.345 Multi-AZ readable standbys (Price List API, AmazonRDS). Apply: social-graph/metadata GB x 0.23 (production should assume Multi-AZ); compute instances are extra and not benchmarked here.

## 2. Egress per GB

- **AWS**: 100 GB/month free; USD 0.090 first 10 TB, 0.085 next 40 TB, 0.070 next 100 TB, 0.050 above 150 TB (AWSDataTransfer price list, effective 2026-06-01). CloudFront differs.
- **GCP**: USD 0.12 (0-1 TB), 0.11 (1-10 TB), 0.08 (10+ TB) to Americas/EMEA (pricing examples).
- **R2**: USD 0. **B2**: free up to 3x average monthly storage, then USD 0.01/GB.
- **Egress per MAU**: UNSOURCED. No platform publishes bytes-served per user. Closest public proxy: Ericsson Mobility Report key figures, 22 GB/month per smartphone in 2025 across all apps (https://www.ericsson.com/en/reports-and-papers/mobility-report/key-figures). Apply: expose as a slider (suggest 1-5 GB/MAU-month for an image-heavy feed, labelled as an assumption), and multiply by the egress rate above.

## 3. Engineer fully loaded cost

- **US**: BLS median annual wage for software developers USD 135,980 (May 2025; https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm). Infra/backend specialists at Levels.fyi run higher but were not fetched; BLS is the defensible default.
- **UK**: ITJobsWatch median advertised Software Developer salary GBP 65,000, six months to 2026-10-21 (https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/uk/software%20developer.do). ONS ASHE not confirmed directly.
- **Germany**: EUR 68.5k-74k median from job-site aggregates (nettocalc, brutto-netto-gehaltsrechner); Low confidence.
- **Multiplier**: BLS ECEC June 2026, private industry: total compensation USD 46.89/hr = wages 32.82 + benefits 14.07; benefits 30.0% of compensation (https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.nr0.htm). 46.89/32.82 = **1.43x** on wages for benefits alone. Apply: fully loaded = base x 1.43; add a separate 10-20% overhead line (equipment, office, software) as an explicit assumption since ECEC excludes it. Default US infra engineer ~ USD 194k/yr.

  - Apply: default 5-15 moderators per 1M MAU; video/ephemeral messaging at the top of the range. These counts cover EU-language capacity only.

## 5. App-store commissions (state as of 2026-10-21)

- **Apple standard 30%**, Small Business Program **15%** for developers under USD 1M prior-year proceeds (https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2020/11/apple-announces-app-store-small-business-program/, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/). Subscriptions: 70% net in year one, **85% after one year of paid service** (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/).
- **Apple EU (DMA), terms effective 2026-10-01** (https://developer.apple.com/support/dma-and-apps-in-the-eu/): IAP 26% (15% reduced for SBP/subs after year one); alternative payment inside app 20% (10%); link-out Store Services Commission 15% (10%), applying to sales within 7 days of link tap; **Core Technology Commission 5%** on alternative marketplaces/web distribution (CTF, Initial Acquisition Fee and Store Services Fee eliminated). Payment choice locked for 12 months.
- **Apple US link-outs**: the 2025-04-30 contempt order bars any commission; Ninth Circuit (No. 25-2935, 2025-12-11) affirmed contempt but held the blanket ban overbroad and remanded to set a cost-based commission; Supreme Court granted review 2026-06-30 and denied a stay; Judge Gonzalez Rogers is running an evidentiary process (Courthouse News, 2026-08-11). Apple filed a proposal for 15% standard / 10% subs-renewal / 5% SBP (TechCrunch 2026-08-14, AppleInsider; pages blocked, from search snippets). **Uncertain**: model US link-outs at 0% today with a 5-15% scenario toggle.
- **Google Play, US/UK/EEA from 2026-06-30** (https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/06/play-expanded-billing.html; https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622): service fee 10% on first USD 1M regardless of billing method; +5% billing fee only if using Play Billing (so 15% effective); above 1M non-subscription: 20% new installs / 25% existing installs (+5%); subscriptions 10% (+5%) at any revenue level. Australia from 2026-09-30, Japan/Korea 2026-12-31, rest of world 2027-09-30.
- **Google Play, other markets until rollout**: 15% first USD 1M, 30% above; subscriptions 15%.

## 7. Breach cost and probability

- IBM Cost of a Data Breach 2026: global average **USD 4.99M** (+12%, record) on IBM's page (https://www.ibm.com/reports/data-breach); US average **USD 11.5M** reported by HIPAA Journal and Security Boulevard quoting the report (IBM page itself does not surface it); mean time to identify and contain 247 days. Per-record cost: not in the 2026 edition page or coverage (UNSOURCED).
- Probability: UNSOURCED. Verizon DBIR 2026 (22,000+ breaches) reports ransomware in ~83% of SMB breaches and small orgs as 96% of ransomware victims, but gives no per-firm annual probability. Hiscox Cyber Readiness Report 2025: 59% of ~6,000 SMEs reported an attack in the prior 12 months (attack != breach). Apply: headline cost is a large-company mean; for a small app use a user-editable probability (suggest 5-15%/yr) and a user-count-scaled severity rather than the USD 4.99M mean.

## 9. Compliance and legal

- **GDPR SME cost**: UNSOURCED at primary level. Vendor (Legiscope, 2026) ranges: micro EUR 5-15k/yr, small 15-50k, medium 50-200k; it attributes "57% of small companies spend EUR 20-45k/yr" to a 2025 DG Justice study that could not be located. The Commission's May 2025 simplification package claims EUR 400M/yr savings across all measures, not GDPR-specific.
- **DPO**: outsourced USD 1,500-8,000/month by complexity (BD Emerson, 2026-07-14, vendor); GDPRLocal packages GBP 600/mo (4 h), 1,080 (8 h), 2,000 (16 h). Apply: USD 2,000/month default.
- **DSAR**: Gartner 2023 Market Guide figure **USD 1,524** per manually handled request, as cited by DataGrail/Mimecast (primary not accessible). Legiscope cites 3.2 requests per 1,000 customers/yr (attributed to DLA Piper 2025; unverified). Apply: requests = MAU x 0.0032/yr x USD 1,524 (or a lower automated cost).
- **EU DSA, non-VLOP**: obligations still apply (point of contact, legal representative if non-EU, T&C transparency, notice-and-action, statement of reasons, annual transparency report; micro/small enterprises exempt from the report and internal complaint system). No per-platform cost in the impact assessment was retrievable; ACT cites a Commission floor of ~EUR 1,500 to set up notice-and-action (2021). UNSOURCED beyond that.
- **UK Online Safety Act** (DSIT enactment IA, Oct 2024, 2019 prices; https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6716222b9242eecc6c849b09/Online_Safety_act_enactment_impact_assessment.pdf): Table 49 transition cost per business: micro GBP 3,720-3,900, small 7,200-7,460, medium 7,000-7,300, large 18,300-18,700 (Table 50 adds ~900 if user reporting must be built). Table 51 ongoing compliance per business: micro/small GBP 87/yr, medium 300, large 599 (Category 1: 390,000). Table 52 including additional moderation and risk assessment: micro/small GBP 6,040 (low/mid risk), small high-risk 50,800, medium mid/high 321,000. Table 19: transparency reporting proxy GBP 45,000/yr (NetzDG). Ofcom fees: QWR threshold GBP 250M, UK-attributable revenue under 10M exempt, fees ~0.02-0.03% of QWR, first charging year 2026/27 (Ofcom July 2025 statement via techUK/Wiggin; Ofcom pages 403). Apply: small app = ~GBP 7,500 one-off + 6,000/yr; Ofcom fee = 0 below threshold.
- **US**: CCPA applies at >USD 25M revenue OR 100k+ CA consumers/households OR 50%+ revenue from selling PI (https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa). COPPA amended rule effective 2025-06-23, compliance deadline 2026-04-22 (https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule). App-store age-verification: Utah SB 142 provider/developer duties effective 2026-05-06, enforcement 2026-12-31; Texas SB 2420 effective 2026-01-01, enjoined 2025-12-23, Fifth Circuit allowed enforcement pending appeal (May 2026), Supreme Court declined to block (2026-07-07). Uncertain and shifting.
